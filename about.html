About Our Dealership

Trusted since 2015 in the heart of Johannesburg

Our Johannesburg Story

Founded in 2015 by Phetole "Phat" Seoka, PHAT's DEALERSHIP began as a small used car lot in the vibrant Braamfontein district of Johannesburg. What started with just five quality pre-owned vehicles and a dream has grown into one of Johannesburg's most trusted automotive destinations.

Our location at 51-55 Juta Street in Braamfontein places us at the heart of Johannesburg's urban revival. We've witnessed the transformation of this historic neighborhood while maintaining our commitment to the community that supported us from the beginning.

The Braamfontein Connection

Being situated in Braamfontein has shaped our business philosophy. We understand the unique needs of Johannesburg drivers - from navigating the bustling city streets to weekend getaways along the Garden Route. Our team knows that every vehicle we sell needs to handle both urban commuting and South Africa's diverse road conditions.
